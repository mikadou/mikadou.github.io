What this PoC does — and does not — prove

The learned policy is trained by imitation from exact solutions. This first experiment asks whether a compact shared network can internalize a recurring structural heuristic and amortize it across unseen instances. It is intentionally not an RL experiment yet.

  • Policy: predicts each next integer value, then the environment clips it to the legal interval implied by the chain.
  • Greedy: picks the legal value closest to the current local target.
  • SA single: traditional single-variable feasible mutations.
  • SA block: a stronger baseline that shifts random contiguous blocks.
  • Exact DP: establishes the global optimum and creates teacher trajectories; it is never called by the policy at inference.

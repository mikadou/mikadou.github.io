What is controlled

Observable proposal quality

Graph state

Nodes receive local margins plus total energy, best energy, violated-edge fraction, recent acceptance, remaining budget, temperature, and stagnation.

Candidate features

Every (node, value) action receives its exact local energy delta, resulting energy, new adjacent violations, SA acceptance probability, and distance from the locally feasible interval.

Training signal

At each visited state, the real objective scores every legal mutation. The policy minimizes expected counterfactual cost with a KL penalty toward uniform exploration. No solution assignment or teacher action is supplied.

Stable horizon

Per-node recurrent memory resets every 32 proposals, and the default training and evaluation envelopes match. The UI warns when testing extrapolates far beyond training.

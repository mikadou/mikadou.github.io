What is controlled

Fair proposal comparison

Shared annealing loop

Both methods use the same temperature schedule and Metropolis acceptance probability. Neither model can bypass rejected moves.

Uniform initialization

The neural output projection starts at zero. All legal mutations therefore have equal probability, and a shared sampler makes the untrained trajectory identical to random SA for the same seed.

Training signal

REINFORCE rewards accepted energy reductions and improvements to the best solution, with a success bonus and small costs for proposals and rejections. No target assignment is supplied.

Evaluation

Both variants start from the same assignment and receive the same proposals and acceptance thresholds before training. After training, only the neural proposal probabilities differ.

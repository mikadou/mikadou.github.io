Growing Neural Cellular Automata

Train a tiny neural cellular automaton directly in this browser tab. This build uses a persistent sample pool, periodic reseeding, and alpha-weighted loss for better convergence.

Weights are saved in IndexedDB. Training-pool states live in RAM and reset when the model resets.

Phone mode starts small. Increase batch/rollout only after the step counter moves smoothly.

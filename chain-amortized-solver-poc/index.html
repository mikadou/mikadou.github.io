Can PPO learn a robust local-repair heuristic?

The learned policy is now compared with a deliberately imperfect handcrafted heuristic. Both get a total budget of 3n variable assignments. The handcrafted solver samples values around 2·i, detects violated adjacent constraints, randomly chooses one of their endpoint variables, and resamples that variable. Simulated annealing remains as a generic search reference.

Handcrafted robust local-repair heuristic

Imperfect construction

For variable xᵢ, sample an integer from a Gaussian with mean 2·i and standard deviation σ=1, clipped to 0…1000. The first n moves independently sample all variables once.

The mean spacing of two deliberately leaves room for sampling errors and ties. This is not the trivial perfect construction xᵢ=i.

Local repair

After the initial assignment, find every violated constraint xᵢ ≥ xᵢ₊₁. Collect the variable nodes that are endpoints of at least one such constraint, pick one uniformly at random, and resample it from its N(2i,1) distribution.

Repeat until the chain is feasible or the total 3n assignment budget is exhausted. A repair may fix one neighboring constraint while breaking another, so robustness matters.

Scale-aware PPO representation

Seven features per node

Variable nodes carry [type-var, type-constraint, absolute position, relative position, problem size, assigned, value]. With MAX_TEST_N=100, the key static features are i/99, i/(n−1), and n/100.

The 8-dimensional encoder remains shared across graph sizes. No raw node-ID bypass is added to the action head.

Mixed-size training

Each PPO episode samples n uniformly between the configured training minimum and maximum. PPO collects 32 episodes per update and self-imitation is disabled.

0 message rounds remains the default diagnostic setting; 1, 2, and 6 rounds can still be tested.

Environment and reward

The PPO environment is unchanged: the actor chooses both variable i and value v, gets at most 3n moves, and receives no per-step feasibility reward. Its terminal reward is the satisfied-constraint fraction, plus a strong complete-solution bonus and an efficiency bonus.

What this comparison tells us

The handcrafted curve is the important target now. If it solves long chains reliably within 3n, then we have demonstrated that a simple stochastic local heuristic with the right structural bias is enough. The learning problem becomes much sharper: can the GNN/PPO policy discover behavior comparable to “focus on violated neighborhoods and sample values with a sensible positional prior?”
